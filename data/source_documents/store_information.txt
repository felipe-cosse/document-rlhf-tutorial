SUNRISE BAKERY CUSTOMER INFORMATION

The bakery is open Tuesday through Friday from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Saturday from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and Sunday from 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. It is closed on Monday.

Standard online orders need at least 24 hours of notice. Custom cakes need at least 10 calendar days of notice. A custom-cake request is not confirmed until a staff member approves the design and the customer pays the deposit.

Customers may collect orders at 42 Garden Avenue. Local delivery is available within 8 miles. The delivery fee is $6 for addresses within 3 miles and $12 for addresses from more than 3 miles through 8 miles.

Products are prepared in a kitchen that handles wheat, milk, eggs, peanuts, and tree nuts. Staff can explain ingredients, but Sunrise Bakery cannot promise that any product is free from cross-contact with an allergen.
