# Sunrise Bakery refund policy

## Fresh food

Fresh food can be replaced or refunded when it is damaged, incorrect, or unsafe. The customer should contact Sunrise Bakery within 24 hours of pickup or delivery and provide the order number and a photo when possible.

We do not refund fresh food only because a customer changed their mind or disliked a flavor that was correctly prepared.

## Custom cakes

Custom-cake deposits are refundable until seven calendar days before the pickup date. After that deadline, the deposit is not refundable because ingredients and decoration work have been scheduled.

If Sunrise Bakery cancels an order, the customer receives a full refund, including the deposit.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds return to the original payment method. Banks normally post the credit within five to ten business days.
